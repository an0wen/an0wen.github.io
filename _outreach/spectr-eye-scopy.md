How does light become color? **Spectr-eye-scopy** is an interactive tool I developed to make spectroscopy approachable through something we experience every day: vision.

The tool connects the spectrum of light reaching the eye to the responses of our three types of cone cells and the colors we perceive. Two interactive views let visitors explore different parts of that story:

- **RGB screens:** adjust red, green, and blue light and luminosity, then compare the resulting color with an illustrative emission spectrum and cone responses.
- **Pigments and illumination:** choose among five pigments and four light sources to see how reflected spectra, cone responses, and the predicted display color change together.

These are illustrative models built from reference spectral data. They provide a way to experiment with the physics of color and to understand why the same object can look different under different lighting.
