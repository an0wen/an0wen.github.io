Past projects and events

Public talks, interviews, school visits, and community astronomy events.

Sharing astronomy beyond the research community is an important part of my work. Through public talks, interviews, school presentations, and informal conversations, I aim to make planetary science accessible and invite questions about the worlds around us.

Community astronomy and schools

  • Astronomy on Tap, UC Santa Cruz (2023–2025): I helped organize events bringing science and astrophysics to the general public.
  • Ask An Astronomer, UC Santa Cruz (2024–2025): I answered astronomy questions submitted by members of the public by email.
  • School presentations (since 2020): visits and presentations to share science and astrophysics with students.
  • Public science events (since 2019): participation in events introducing astronomy to wider audiences.
