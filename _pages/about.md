Hi, my name is Artem Aguichine, and I am currently an Associate Professor at the Instituto de Astronomía - Ensenada, UNAM (IA - Ens, UNAM).

I am a planetary scientist, and my research focuses on connecting theory of planet formation, evolution, and interior structure to measurable properties of (exo)planets to infer their composition.

I am particularly interested in the distribution of <a href='https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CHNOPS'>volatile species</a> in our Solar System, and the presence of water in <a href='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/an0wen/an0wen.github.io/refs/heads/main/assets/img/sub-Neptunes.png'>sub-Neptunes</a>. My research is connected to the broader field of astrobiology, shedding light on the distribution of life throughout planetary systems and the origin of life in our own Solar System.

I am the founder and coordinator of the [PEARL database](https://pearl-database-demo.onrender.com/).
