I am committed to raising awareness about climate change and to sharing the science behind it. Studying other planets offers a powerful perspective on Earth: understanding distant worlds also reminds us of the importance of protecting our own.

I am a member of [Astronomers for Planet Earth (A4E)](https://a4e.org/), a community of astronomers working to address the climate crisis through education, public engagement, and action within our profession.
